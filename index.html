Meet,

Jithin George, a Mechanical and Robotics Engineer with a Master’s in Mechatronics, Automation, and Robotics from NYU Tandon and a Bachelor’s in Mechanical Engineering. My expertise lies at the intersection of mechanical design, automation, and embedded systems. I have hands-on experience designing and developing robotic systems, integrating control architectures, and automating industrial processes. My work spans from designing humanoid and hexapod robots to implementing industrial automation with Allen-Bradley PLCs and building manufacturable mechanical components optimized for real-world use. I’m passionate about creating intelligent, efficient, and impactful solutions — whether it’s a complex robotic system or a streamlined automation workflow. I thrive on solving engineering challenges and bringing innovative ideas to life through thoughtful design, prototyping, and testing. On this site, you’ll find highlights of my projects, skills, and experience. Feel free to reach out — I’m always excited to collaborate and contribute to meaningful engineering and robotics endeavors.
