Electric Solar Vehicle Competition

Chromoly roll cage body with an anticipated weight of 18 kg provides maximum security for the driver and guarantees the integrity of the frame under all demanding conditions, while offering improved ergonomics, flexible mounting positions, and optimal torsional rigidity. The steering system is engineered to turn the wheels with minimal driver effort via a suitable overall ratio, ensuring precise handling and drivability even under extreme track conditions.

The transmission is designed to transfer sufficient power to the wheels to overcome any obstacle encountered during the event, with every component conceived, manufactured, and installed according to best engineering practices. Meanwhile, the suspension’s ultimate aim is to maintain maximum rubber contact with the ground to maximize performance, delivering a stable and enjoyable ride for the racer.
