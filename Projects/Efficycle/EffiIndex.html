SAE: Efficycle 2019

EFFI-CYCLE is an intercollegiate design challenge in which undergraduate teams conceive and build a fully human-powered, three-wheeled vehicle that excels in aerodynamics, engineering precision, ergonomics, and consumer appeal—balancing visual design with performance, reliability, and ease of operation. Beyond simply crafting an energy-efficient machine, participants gain hands-on experience tackling real-world problems, collaborating across disciplines, practicing design for manufacturability, and managing the complete product life cycle—all while championing eco-friendly transportation solutions in India.

Teams must design, analyze, validate, and fabricate a three-wheeled vehicle capable of being driven either simultaneously or alternately by two riders. Each entry is evaluated on both its engineering merits and on-track performance, with cumulative scores across all events determining the final standings. Importantly, every aspect of research, design, and construction must be carried out exclusively by the current student members of each college, ensuring full team engagement and learning throughout the process.
